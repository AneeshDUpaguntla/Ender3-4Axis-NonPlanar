# Day 1: Initial planning and printer assessment

I started by looking over the Ender 3 frame, motion system, and overall build volume to determine whether a 4th-axis conversion would be practical. The main goal was to understand how much of the existing printer geometry I could keep without introducing excessive flex or interference.

I measured the printer carefully and checked how much space is available around the hotend, bed, and gantry for a rotary axis. This was an important first step because the extra axis is not just another part to add; it changes the working envelope and can easily collide with the machine if the geometry is not planned correctly.
